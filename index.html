Who we are

A framing and drywall crew that shows up, squares up, and closes the file.

Integrated Framing & Drywall — IFD — is a fully licensed and insured framing company working across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. We frame new home builds, commercial projects and industrial projects, then carry the walls through to drywall, taping and finish.

Clients come back to us — builders, general contractors, architects and homeowners — because we treat every wall the same way: plumb, level, on spec, and ready for the next trade. Fast results, without the trade-off on workmanship.

  • Licensed & insured

    Fully covered for residential, commercial and industrial work.

  • Level and plumb

    Walls that read flat under raking light — ready for paint.

  • Fast results

    Sequenced to keep your other trades moving on site.

What we do

Five scopes. One accountable crew.

From the first stud to the final sand, IFD handles the wall assemblies that make an interior feel finished.

  1. 01

    Steel stud framing

    Metal stud framing in Toronto and the GTA — new home builds, commercial finishes and industrial projects, from a single partition to full floor framing.

    • Load-bearing & non-load-bearing
    • Bulkheads & soffits
    • Shaft walls
  2. 02

    Drywall

    Board installed fast and hung clean, so construction time comes down and the finish coat has something true to sit on.

    • Standard & fire-rated board
    • Moisture-resistant
    • Ceilings & bulkheads
  3. 03

    Taping & sanding

    Joints taped, feathered and sanded by hand. This is the stage that decides whether a wall disappears or telegraphs every seam — it’s not a job for a shortcut.

    • Paper & mesh tape
    • Multi-coat finishing
    • Paint-ready sanding
  4. 04

    Sound insulation

    Hard, smooth surfaces reflect sound. Acoustic batts are installed to absorb and block it — quieter rooms, less noise transfer between spaces.

    • Partition & party walls
    • Ceiling assemblies
    • Home theatres & offices

Plus the work around the wall

Renovation framing, patch-and-repair, attic and basement insulation, and light demolition ahead of the rebuild — ask us what your scope needs.

How it runs

Four steps, no surprises.

We stay in constant communication with our customers until the job is done.

  1. 01

    Walk the space

    Tell us the address, the scope and the drawings. We take a look and tell you what the walls actually need.

  2. 02

    Quote the scope

    A free, itemised quote — framing, board, tape, insulation — so you can see where the money goes.

  3. 03

    Frame & board

    The crew is sequenced with your other trades. Studs, blocking and board go up to spec, on schedule.

  4. 04

    Tape & hand over

    Joints feathered, sanded and inspected. You get paint-ready walls and a direct line to the crew.
